# Import the purchase documents of the tax years 2021 to 2024

## Why

The 2025 documents were imported on 10 and 11 October 2026 and reconciled against the tax draft.
The owner wants the same for the four years before, so the app holds the whole history of the
business and the finance area can show a year next to the one before it.

## What the survey found (2026-10-11, read-only)

| Year | Document files (PDF) | Profit statement filed | Declared business expenses | of which depreciation | Assessment |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2024 | 211 | 4 August 2025 | 3,420.00 | 650.22 | present |
| 2023 | 135 | yes (portal export) | 2,842.72 | 1,291.89 | present, subject to review |
| 2022 | 188 | 4 October 2023 | 4,769.14 | 1,291.89 | present, provisional for the business income |
| 2021 | 114 (97 distinct) | 22 October 2022 | 4,525.44 | 1,175.65 | none on disk |

- The profit statement is the "Anlage EÜR" (income-surplus statement); the employment annex is
  the "Anlage N", which carried the study costs (work equipment 853 in 2023 and 782 in 2024; the
  figures of 2022 and 2021 are read from the filed returns when the year is started).
- The app holds no receipt for any of the four years. The finance area lists the years, counts
  0 receipts in each and computes them with the 2025 rule set, which it says on the page. 2024
  has 135 payments from the bank import of 2025, the other years 3 each.
- Rule sets exist for 2025 and 2026 only (`src/lib/tax/rules`). The form of 2024 uses the same
  line numbers as 2025 for every line that was declared (33 depreciation, 36 low-value assets,
  43 telecommunication, 45 training, 51 work equipment, 62 gifts). 2023 is shifted by one or two
  lines (32, 35, 41, 43, 48, 49), 2022 and 2021 use the older, longer form (total on line 88).
- The owner's own expense table of those years is in the Notion export
  (`Obsidian/Notion Export/.../Freelancing/Taxes`, table "business expenses", 198 rows): per
  item the gross amount, the business share, the claimed amount, the expense type and whether
  it was checked against the tax portal. It is the bridge between single documents and the
  summed lines of a return. Its claimed sums do not equal the declared totals to the cent in any
  year; the difference per year is worked out in that year's log and is a finding, not something
  the import corrects.
- The returns often declared a year of monthly invoices as one line, and the Notion export holds
  merged files for that ("combined invoices 2021"). The year folders hold the single invoices.

## Decisions

1. **Every year is a record of what was declared.** All four are filed. Assignment and share are
   set from the declared table; a document that was never declared is imported without a claim.
   "Without a claim" means the assignment stays empty: the receipt is an open check that counts
   nothing, never "Privat", which nobody has said. An assignment the reader set from the document
   itself stays and is listed as a difference to the return.
   Nothing here changes a return.
2. **One receipt per document file, as in 2025.** Single invoices are uploaded, merged copies are
   not. A file with the same content as one already uploaded is skipped and logged.
   A merged file without single invoices next to it is split into its invoices outside the
   folder and those are uploaded. An order confirmation is not uploaded when the invoice of the
   same order is there; a file of empty pages and an invoice over 0.00 are not receipts.
3. **Business and study documents go under Whiz-Art Media**, where the finance area counts both
   the profit statement and the employment annex. Documents filed as private and never declared
   go under "marlinjai", as in 2025.
4. **Form lines only for 2024.** For 2023, 2022 and 2021 the receipts are imported with vendor,
   date, total, assignment and share, and no form line, because the app would print a 2025 line
   number next to a figure of another form. Rule sets for those years are a separate piece of
   work and stay on this plan's roadmap line until they have their own.
5. **The rules of 2025 hold**: no purchase refunded in full, no receipt billed to another person,
   no contracts or applications, no team subscriptions of the story-app startup.
6. **Payments are out of scope.** Bank and payment-service files of those years are not imported
   here; the receipts are the record. Depreciation of assets bought before 2021 is not rebuilt.
7. **No deletes** except rows created by mistake in the same batch.

## How

Newest year first, one year finished and reported before the next:

1. Extraction table for the year next to its tax folder (`kaufbelege-<year>-extracted.csv`):
   one row per file with vendor, date, total, content hash, the matching row of the declared
   table and the decision (import under which company, or skip with the reason).
2. Snapshot of the finance area for that year and for 2025 before the first upload.
3. Upload in small batches through `/app`, so the reader runs. After each batch, from a fresh
   load: compare vendor, date and total with the extraction table, correct what is wrong, set
   assignment and share, and for 2024 the form line.
4. Log per year (`import-log-<year>.md`): every row created or changed with old and new value,
   how the reader did (right, flagged, wrong), skipped files with the reason.
5. Snapshot after. The year's business expenses in the app against the declared total without
   depreciation, the difference explained row by row. The 2025 figures (14,074.75 business
   expenses, 257 receipts counted on 2026-10-11) must be unchanged by this work; the owner is
   entering business meals of 2025 himself, so a change there is checked against meal receipts
   before it is treated as a defect.

## Progress

- **2024, done 2026-10-11.** 211 files, 170 distinct, 156 receipts in the app. Form lines against
  the return: 36 low-value assets 697.23 and 697.23; 43 telecommunication 394.95 and 395.06; 45
  training 257.98 and 258.20; 51 work equipment 1,398.62 and 1,399.31. In the app and not in the
  return: one invoice of 256.70 the reader took as business. 38 receipts without an assignment
  (not declared). Log, file table and snapshot are in the owner's tax folder of 2024.
- **2023, done 2026-10-11.** 135 files, 95 distinct, 148 receipts (70 of them split out of merged
  yearly files). Without depreciation the return declared 1,550.83, the app counts 1,714.76: the
  difference of 163.93 is telecommunication (the documents hold more landline than the return's
  basis, 141.63) and extra items on one invoice (22.75). Training, software and work equipment
  match within 0.45. 16 receipts without an assignment, among them a laptop invoice of 943.00
  that is in no line of the return.
- **2022, done 2026-10-11.** 188 files, 126 distinct, 119 receipts. Without depreciation and meals
  the return declared 3,393.25; the documents give 3,450.98 once two open checks are decided (a
  used lens above the low-value limit and a scanned invoice): 57.73 more, of which 63.91 is one
  invoice the return carried net. Employment annex 1,607.99 in the app against 2,172 declared
  (fees without a document, the study share of mixed subscriptions, the laptop at full price).
- **2021, done 2026-10-11.** 114 files, 97 distinct, 91 receipts. The return declared 4,525.44, the
  app counts 5,129.14: 603.70 more, explained line by line (assets without a schedule 301.04,
  shipping inside two invoices, dollar invoices on estimated rates) except running software costs,
  where the documents hold 379.37 more than the return and no subset of them gives the declared
  figure.
- Per year the log (`import-log-<year>.md`), the file table and the snapshot are in the owner's
  tax folder of that year.

## How it went, for the next import

- Decision 3 was narrowed while working: only documents the owner filed as private go under the
  private company. A folder named "not declared" says not declared, so those documents are under
  Whiz-Art Media without an assignment, like every other document a return did not carry.
- 2023, 2022 and 2021 were given to three workers at once. The uploads ran, but the browser tool
  takes one script at a time for the whole machine, so the workers queued behind each other and
  blocked other sessions for about an hour. Browser steps belong to one worker at a time.
- What is left is on the roadmap as lines of its own: rule sets for 2021 to 2024, asset
  schedules, shares on both forms, and the reader's mistakes found here.

## After the import: the years have their own rule sets (2026-10-15)

The rule sets of 2021 to 2024 now exist (see the finance plan, "Reality: rule sets for
2021 to 2024"). Decision 4 above no longer holds back the form lines of 2023, 2022 and
2021: they can be set in the finance area, which now prints each year's own line numbers,
and the business and study shares of 2022 and 2023 can be recorded on both forms.
